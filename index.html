Certification Compass — UAE edition

How to read this

Ratings (out of 10) weigh UAE hiring demand, exam rigour, cost-to-value and shelf life. Prices are list exam fees as of October 2026, shown in AED or USD (switch in the top bar). AED is converted from USD at the fixed 3.6725 peg, except Microsoft, which uses its published UAE price (AED 365 fundamentals / AED 600 associate & expert). Pearson VUE may add 5% UAE VAT; ISACA and IAPP charge less to members; "~" marks prices that vary by provider or include a mandatory course.

Most exams can be taken online or at Pearson VUE / PSI centres in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah. Exam catalogues change often — 2026 alone saw AWS retire ML Specialty and Advanced Networking, and Microsoft replace AZ-204, AI-102, AZ-500 and DP-100. Confirm the price on the exam page before you book.
